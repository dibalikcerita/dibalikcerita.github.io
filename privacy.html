Privacy Policy

Di Balik Cerita Creator Studio ("the app") is a private publishing tool used only by the team behind the Di Balik Cerita channels to publish their own short videos to their own social media accounts, including TikTok. It is not offered to the public. Last updated: 28 November 2026.

Information we access

  • From TikTok, after the account owner authorizes the app: the account's open ID, display name, avatar and the posting options TikTok returns (privacy levels, interaction settings, maximum video length).
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, so the app can publish when the owner presses Post.
  • Status of each video the owner publishes through the app.

We do not access followers, messages, viewing history, contacts or any data of other TikTok users.

How we use it

Only to show the owner who they are posting as, to offer TikTok's own posting options, and to upload and publish videos the owner chooses. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling or sale.

Storage and sharing

Tokens and account details are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a private folder that is never published. We do not sell, rent or share this information with anyone. Videos are sent only to TikTok's official API.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while the TikTok account stays connected. The owner can disconnect at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions); the stored tokens are then deleted from the app.

Children

The app is operated by adults for their own accounts and does not collect data from children.

Contact

Questions about this policy: send a message to @dibalikcerita.studio on Instagram or to our Facebook page.
